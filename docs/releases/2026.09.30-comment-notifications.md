# Comment Management 1.3.0 optional-plugin release

The shared plugin adds administrator-controlled, per-site email recipient lists
for published and awaiting-moderation comments/product reviews. Existing native
recipients, notification gates, comment policy and permissions are preserved.
WooCommerce review moderation recipients are extended after its override.
