## Cohorts and stopping

Only after all canary evidence passes and cohort authorization is explicit, qualify a small cohort of comparable child-theme Stack sites individually. Then expand to remaining compatible sites in bounded cohorts; derived-child, optional-plugin and legacy variants must qualify on their own evidence. Fresh targeted sync, preflight and verified pre-write backup are mandatory for each site. Stop the entire next cohort on any failed backup, checksum, readiness, functional, accessibility, performance or verification gate. Record the exact failing site and do not retry writes blindly after timeouts; read back first.

## Rollback

Keep the prior installed immutable lock and its exact source/package bytes, backup receipt and deployment-agent recovery record before apply. A site's prior release may differ from the preceding source lock. Use the supported rollback plan for that site's actual previous lock and verified target backups, preserving the child theme. Restore database only when required by a verified data migration/failure and separately authorized; do not overwrite unrelated intervening content. Recheck old lock/component hashes, editor/frontend behavior, REST, logs and accessibility. An incomplete rollback blocks all further cohorts and requires investigation through the authorized deployment workflow.
